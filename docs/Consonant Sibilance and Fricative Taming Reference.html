Consonant Sibilance & Fricative Taming Reference

A focused table of consonants, frequency bands, and envelope shapes for transparent vocal control.

Overview

This document provides a practical reference for taming sibilance, fricatives, and plosives in vocal recordings. It focuses on:

  • Consonant type – what you are controlling.
  • Primary frequency band – where the energy is concentrated.
  • EQ move – suggested filter type, center frequency, Q, and gain range.
  • Envelope shape – how gain reduction should ramp in and out.
  • Timing – typical attack and release windows that feel natural.

All values are typical ranges, not rigid rules. Adjust by ear for the specific voice, mic, and mix context.

Envelope Shape Definitions

5‑Point S‑Curve

Smooth, natural, ideal for S and SH.

3‑Point Wedge

Fast and simple, great for F, K, T.

4‑Point Micro‑Curve

Quick grab, smooth release.

Downward Spike Clamp

Instant clamp for plosives like P.

Single‑Point Clamp

Minimal, on/off style reduction.
